AudoAck

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

In short: AudoAck handles account information, device/session information, audio recordings submitted for analysis, and analysis results. Audio is used to provide the recording and analysis features.

1. About AudoAck

AudoAck is an Android application and related web service for recording, submitting, and reviewing audio analysis results. This policy explains what information AudoAck handles, how it is used, how it may be shared, and how you can request deletion.

2. Information we handle

  • Account information: username and authentication information required to sign in.
  • Device and session information: device identifiers, device tokens, session and batch identifiers, timestamps, and last-seen information used to associate activity with an authenticated account and device.
  • Audio: audio recordings or audio chunks that you explicitly record or submit for analysis.
  • Analysis results: derived information such as emotional tone, intensity, background-noise indicators, audio quality, speaker-overlap indicators, silence indicators, and confidence values.

3. Microphone and audio recording

The Android application requests microphone access because recording audio is a core feature. Before recording, the app should explain why microphone access is needed and request the Android microphone permission. Audio captured for an active recording session is transmitted to the AudoAck service when it is submitted for analysis.

Do not record people or conversations unless you have the right, permission, or other lawful basis to do so.

4. How information is used

  • Authenticate users and maintain signed-in sessions.
  • Associate recordings with devices, sessions, and batches.
  • Receive and process submitted audio.
  • Generate and display audio analysis results.
  • Display the latest available results for connected devices.
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and operate the service.

5. Sharing and service providers

AudoAck does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure, hosting, storage, security, and other service providers that are necessary to operate the application. Such processing is limited to the purposes required to provide and maintain the service and is subject to applicable contractual and security controls.

6. Retention

Recordings, account information, device information, and analysis data may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain associated records, comply with applicable obligations, and resolve support or security issues.

7. Data deletion requests

You may request deletion of your AudoAck account and associated personal information by contacting the service owner through the contact details provided on the Google Play listing or the AudoAck service website. We may need to verify the request before processing it.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate operational purposes.

8. Security

AudoAck uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. Data transmitted between the application and the service is intended to use encrypted HTTPS connections. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

AudoAck is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the service, contact the service owner so the information can be reviewed and, where appropriate, deleted.

10. Your responsibilities when recording audio

You are responsible for ensuring that your use of AudoAck complies with applicable privacy, recording, employment, and consent laws. Do not use the application to secretly record or unlawfully collect another person's private communications.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when AudoAck's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page with its updated date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, account-related privacy requests, or deletion requests, use the contact information published with the AudoAck application on Google Play or on the AudoAck service website.
