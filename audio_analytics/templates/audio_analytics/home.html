The problem

Your QA team cannot listen to every call. Your customers still expect every call to matter.

Manual call review samples a small percentage of conversations. By the time a supervisor finds frustration, an escalation or a recurring issue, the opportunity to intervene may already be gone.

What gets missed
• Frustrated or unhappy customers
• Escalation-risk conversations
• Agent coaching opportunities
• Repeated customer problems
• Poor audio quality affecting CX
The AudoAck platform

Automate the work around call QA—not the human judgment that matters.

AudoAck is designed to give QA and operations teams a continuous intelligence layer across customer conversations.

🎙️

Conversation ingestion

Bring customer-call audio into an authenticated, device-aware workflow for batch or ongoing processing.

🧠

AI analysis

Extract conversation and audio signals such as emotional tone, intensity, noise, quality, overlap and silence.

🚨

QA intelligence

Turn analysis into risk and QA signals so teams can prioritize the conversations that need human attention.

End-to-end workflow

From customer call to actionable QA signal.
